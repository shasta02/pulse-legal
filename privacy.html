Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Flare is built to share as little as possible, for as short a time as possible. This policy explains what Flare collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes. Friends never see it.
  • Your profile: the name and avatar color you choose, and your friend code.
  • Your friends and circles: who you're friends with, pending requests, and the circles you make. Circles are private to you; people aren't told which circles they're in.
  • Beacons you light: the activity, an optional note, the neighborhood, a coarse area (about 1 km across), the times, who it's for, and its look.
  • Joins and chat: who said they're coming, messages in a beacon's chat, and, only if you tap "I'm here", your precise location at that moment.
  • Reports and blocks you make, including a copy of a reported message or beacon note.
  • Flare+ status. Apple handles payment. We never see your card or Apple Account details.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your location is used on your iPhone to work out your neighborhood and distances. Only the neighborhood and coarse area are sent, except the "I'm here" pin above.
  • Your calendar, if you connect it. Flare reads only when you're busy, to suggest free windows. Event titles, people and places are never read or sent.
  • Your likes and saved spots.
  • Idea suggestions are made by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

Who can see what

  • Your beacons are visible only to accepted friends you send them to.
  • A beacon's chat, joins and pins are visible only to the host and the people who joined.
  • People who join the same beacon can see each other's name and avatar while it's live.
  • If you block someone, you stop seeing each other's beacons and messages.

How long we keep it

  • Beacons, joins, pins and chats are deleted automatically within about a minute of the beacon ending. They aren't archived.
  • Your account, friends and circles are kept until you delete your account.
  • Reports are kept while we review them and for as long as needed to keep Flare safe.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics SDKs. We use these service providers only to run Flare:

  • Supabase: hosts the database and sign-in.
  • Brevo: sends sign-in code emails.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and on-device features.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and avatar color in Account.
  • Turn off location or calendar access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account in Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, friends, circles, beacons and chats right away.
  • To get a copy of your data, or with any privacy question, contact us at pulsebeaconapp@gmail.com.

Children

Flare isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
