Help & contact

Email pulsebeaconapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within a day.

Report someone

In the app, press and hold a message or a person, or tap ⋯ on a beacon, then choose Report. We review every report within 24 hours. If you're in danger, call your local emergency number first.

Block someone

Same menus, choose Block. You stop being friends and won't see each other's beacons or messages. They aren't told. Unblock in Account → Blocked people.

Didn't get your sign-in code?

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from Flare, and mark it "Not spam". Codes expire after an hour; you can ask for a new one.

Delete your account

Account → Delete account. Everything is deleted right away and can't be recovered.

Flare+

Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
